Kaylee Pascarella

PhD Statistics · Austin, Texas

I’m a PhD statistician recently graduated from Baylor University. Most of my training is in Bayesian methods and clinical trial estimands. My favorite part of the work is that it’s collaborative: the interesting problems arrive from someone else, in someone else’s language, and I get to use my statistical background to help solve them.

I defended my PhD in Statistics at Baylor University in July 2026, advised by Dr. John W. Seaman Jr. My dissertation developed Bayesian methods for two stages of drug development: analyzing composite strategy estimands in confirmatory trials, and estimating drug exposure in preclinical studies where each animal contributes only a single measurement.

Alongside the methodology, I have spent several years doing applied work. I interned with AstraZeneca’s Late Respiratory and Immunology statistics group, giving me exposure to the real clinical trial process and its common problems. I have supported clinical research at Baylor Scott & White Health, and I consulted through Baylor’s Statistical Consulting Center on problems ranging from municipal infrastructure to public health. I work mainly in R, alongside SAS and Python.
